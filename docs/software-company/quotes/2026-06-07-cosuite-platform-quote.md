## Executive Summary

co-Suite is not a simple post generator. It is an AI marketing operating system for businesses, creators, and agencies. The product needs account signup, Suite onboarding, business/brand memory, multilingual UX, content generation, image/video/carousel generation, product bulk generation, approval workflows, publishing, scheduling, Meta/Google integrations, analytics, billing/credits, queues, media storage, admin monitoring, SEO pages, and later mobile apps.

The recommended build path is phased:

1. **Production MVP**: stabilize the current web/API product and make the core Suite, generation, connections, and review workflows reliable.
2. **Platform V1**: complete the operating system: campaigns, scheduling, billing, admin, stronger analytics, social calendar loops, and scalable queues.
3. **Scale + Mobile**: iOS/Android apps, multi-tenant agency features, advanced automation, and deeper ad platform management.

## Subscription And Credit Model Assumption

The product model should support:

- Monthly or yearly subscription, for example starting around `$14.99/month`.
- Included generation tokens per plan, such as average daily image usage and video every few days.
- Token bundles for heavier usage.
- Plan upgrades for agencies or higher generation frequency.
- Separate advertising budget balance for Meta/Google/TikTok campaigns.
- Clear queue states when AI/provider limits are hit.

## Assumptions

- Current codebase remains the starting point; this quote is for hardening and completing it, not rebuilding from zero.
- Existing Railway deployment, FastAPI backend, Next.js frontend, DB, and R2 direction remain valid.
- AI providers stay as a multi-provider system: Anthropic for ideas/copy where desired, OpenAI for Suite/onboarding testing, Google/Gemini/Veo for image/video where appropriate.
- Meta and Google permissions may require app review or business verification for production-grade access.
- Exact mobile scope should be defined after web platform stabilizes.
- Legal copy should be reviewed by a lawyer before public launch.

## Scope Excluded

- Actual advertising budgets.
- AI API usage costs.
- Third-party subscription fees.
- Legal counsel fees.
- Paid app store accounts.
- Professional brand strategy workshops beyond product UX/design work.
- Full CRM, email marketing, WhatsApp automation, and TikTok Ads management unless added in later scope.

## Key Risks And Buffers

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI provider limits or outages | Users may wait for generation. | Queue jobs, retry logic, provider status, admin alerts, fallback models. |
| Meta/Google permission restrictions | Analytics/publishing/campaign management may be limited. | Permission matrix, staged integration, app review preparation. |
| Video generation cost and latency | Poor UX if requests block or fail silently. | Async jobs, previews, visible cost/credit use, provider mode selection. |
| Multilingual generation quality | Arabic/Hebrew text can fail in images/videos. | Model-specific rules, fallback text strategy, preview/review loop. |
| Scope growth | Product can expand endlessly. | Milestones, quote control, feature flags, release gates. |
| Mobile too early | Duplicates broken web flows. | Stabilize web workflows first, then build mobile around proven flows. |
