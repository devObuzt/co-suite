## 1. Title

# co-Suite

## The AI marketing operating system for every business

co-Suite turns a business's website, social pages, brand assets, products, audience, and connected ad accounts into a living marketing workspace that can generate, review, schedule, publish, and learn.

---

## 2. The Problem

# Small businesses do not have a marketing operating system

Most local businesses, creators, and agencies are stuck between three bad options:

- Hiring expensive teams before they can prove growth.
- Using generic AI tools that do not understand the business, language, brand, audience, or market.
- Manually switching between Instagram, Facebook, Google Ads, design tools, spreadsheets, calendars, and messaging apps.

The result: inconsistent content, weak campaign discipline, slow execution, and no persistent marketing memory.

---

## 3. The Insight

# The bottleneck is not content generation. It is business understanding.

Generic AI can create a post. It cannot reliably know:

- What this business sells.
- Who the audience is.
- Which languages matter.
- What the brand should sound and look like.
- Which offers, campaigns, competitors, and platforms matter.
- What the user approved, rejected, edited, or corrected last time.

co-Suite starts by building the business brain, then uses it across every marketing action.

---

## 4. The Solution

# One suite per business. One memory. Many workflows.

A Suite is the workspace and memory layer for a business or client.

It contains:

- Business profile.
- Brand profile.
- Audience and language profile.
- Products and services.
- USP, ESP, marketing messages, and content rules.
- Logos, colors, fonts, personas, and reference images.
- Platform connections and analytics.
- Generated content, campaigns, schedules, and feedback.

Every generation, campaign, and report becomes context-aware by default.

---

## 5. Product Experience

# From onboarding to operating rhythm

1. User creates an account and selects type: business, creator, or agency.
2. User creates a Suite and provides website/social links.
3. co-Suite reads public sources and extracts business intelligence.
4. User confirms or edits profile steps: category, languages, services, audience, USP/ESP, brand assets, personas.
5. User generates content, product creatives, videos, carousels, ads, campaigns, and calendar loops.
6. User approves, rejects, edits, schedules, publishes, or marks content as used.
7. Feedback becomes future rules inside Suite Memory.

---

## 6. Why Now

# AI creation is becoming cheap. Context and workflow are the defensible layer.

AI models are improving fast, especially for multilingual text, image generation, video generation, and structured reasoning.

But small businesses still need:

- A place that remembers the business.
- A workflow that matches how marketing actually gets done.
- Native Arabic, Hebrew, English, and global language support.
- Human approval and control.
- Connections to the ad and publishing platforms where work goes live.

The winning product is not one model. It is the operating layer around many models and platforms.

## 12. Architecture

# Built as a Suite-centric AI workflow platform

High-level system:

- Next.js web app.
- FastAPI backend.
- Suite, ContentPost, GenerationJob, ProductBulk models.
- AI providers: OpenAI and Anthropic for text/reasoning; Google image/video generation paths.
- External platforms: Meta, Instagram, Facebook, Google Ads, R2 storage.
- Roadmap: job-first generation architecture and SuiteContextBuilder as the canonical context layer.

The architecture direction is clear: every workflow reads from Suite Memory and writes back learnings.
