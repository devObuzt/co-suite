co-Suite
Confidential investor narrative · June 2026

The AI marketing operating system for every business

co-Suite turns a business's website, social pages, brand assets, products, audience, and connected ad accounts into a living marketing workspace.

co-Suite
Problem

Small businesses do not have a marketing operating system.

Too expensive

Hiring a full marketing team is out of reach before the business proves growth.

Too generic

AI tools generate content, but do not understand the business, language, brand, audience, or market.

Too fragmented

Work is scattered across social apps, ad managers, design tools, calendars, spreadsheets, and chat.

co-Suite
Insight

The bottleneck is not content generation. It is business understanding.

Generic AI can create a post. It cannot reliably know what this business sells, who the audience is, what language to use, what the brand looks like, or what the user approved last time.

Without memory

Every prompt starts from zero. Quality depends on how well the user explains the business again.

With Suite Memory

Every post, image, video, campaign, and schedule is grounded in the business profile, brand, audience, and feedback history.

co-Suite
Solution

One suite per business. One memory. Many workflows.

Business brain

Business profile, category, services, products, audience, USP, ESP, and marketing message.

Brand brain

Logos, colors, fonts, visual rules, personas, content tone, language rules, and feedback learnings.

Operating brain

Connected platforms, analytics, generated content, calendars, campaigns, product bulk jobs, publishing status.

co-Suite
Product flow

From onboarding to daily marketing operations.

1. Build the Suite

User provides website and social links. co-Suite reads sources, extracts business intelligence, and guides profile confirmation step by step.

2. Operate the Suite

User generates, reviews, edits, approves, schedules, publishes, analyzes, and improves future rules from feedback.

3. Expand workflows

Product creatives, carousels, videos, sponsored campaigns, social calendar loops, and agency multi-client operations.

4. Keep learning

Every approval, rejection, upload, campaign, and platform connection strengthens the Suite Memory.

co-Suite
Why now

AI creation is becoming cheap. Context and workflow are the defensible layer.

  • Text, image, and video generation models are improving quickly.
  • Small businesses still need control, approvals, scheduling, platform connections, and analytics.
  • Multilingual local markets need Arabic, Hebrew, English, and additional languages as native product experiences.
  • The winning product is not one model. It is the operating layer around many models and platforms.
