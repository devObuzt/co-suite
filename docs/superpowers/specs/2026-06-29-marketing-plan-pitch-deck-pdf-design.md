# Marketing Plan Pitch Deck PDF Design

## Goal

Redesign the generated marketing plan PDF so it feels like a persuasive client-facing pitch deck, not an exported report. The file should show the full marketing picture: business context, market reading, keywords, competitors, demand/supply, customer personas, strategic direction, and next steps.

The deck must support Arabic, Hebrew, and English content with correct RTL/LTR rendering and no broken glyphs.

## Reference Direction

The attached reference PDFs use a consistent deck language:

- Landscape 16:9 pages, close to 900 x 507 pt.
- Dark premium backgrounds with one strong accent color.
- Cover pages split between a bold text panel and a large contextual image.
- Large typography, short persuasive copy, and low-density content.
- Section cards, chips, and dashboard blocks instead of long lists.
- The deck is built to convince a client, not only document data.

## Output Format

- PDF, landscape 16:9.
- Target length: 16-20 pages when enough data exists.
- If a section has little or no data, keep the page polished and show a concise "not generated yet" state instead of omitting the page unexpectedly.
- Page text must use the audience/application language, with RTL layout for Arabic and Hebrew.

## Content Structure

1. Cover
   - Business/suite name.
   - Category and market.
   - Short strategic subtitle.
   - Context chips such as language, country, audience, and generation date.
   - Optional contextual hero image or a strong visual panel.

2. Business Snapshot
   - Cards for brand name, market, audience, language, location, and project nature.
   - Short paragraph explaining what the plan is built to solve.

3. Services and Products
   - Group services into 2-6 cards.
   - Avoid long bullets. If there are many services, split across multiple pages.

4. Market Reading
   - Summarize the opportunity, problem, competition pressure, and suggested positioning.
   - Use three to four insight cards.

5. Keywords
   - Group keywords by intent where possible:
     - Direct purchase/service intent.
     - Learning/research intent.
     - Comparison intent.
     - Local intent.
   - Show the most useful terms first.

6. Competitors
   - Group by source: Google Organic, Google Maps, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok.
   - Each competitor card should include name, source, short description, and a compact URL.
   - Explain why the competitor matters as a market signal.

7. Demand and Supply
   - Use a dashboard page for average demand, competition, market pressure, and Google suggestions.
   - Follow with a compact table or cards for the highest-signal keywords.

8. Customer Personas
   - Show 2-3 personas per page.
   - Each card includes name, age, gender, economic status, profession, challenge, motivation, and how the offer solves it.

9. Strategic Direction
   - Recommended marketing message.
   - Suggested channels.
   - Differentiation angle.
   - Content themes.

10. 30/60/90 Day Execution
    - Three columns or cards.
    - Make the next actions feel concrete and sellable.

11. Closing Page
    - Short confident summary.
    - Generated by OneShare/Cosuite.

## Visual System

- Background: premium charcoal/navy with subtle lighter panels.
- Accent: dynamic color selected from business tone when available; fallback pink/violet.
- Typography: large titles, medium section headings, concise body text.
- Cards: low radius, clear borders, enough padding.
- Page layout: consistent margins, page number, and small section label.
- Use empty space intentionally; avoid dense table-like report pages.

## Data And Fallbacks

- Use saved suite data only; do not trigger new generation during PDF download.
- If data is missing, show a designed placeholder page that explains what stage must be generated.
- Links should be compact but human-readable.
- Long text must be clamped or split across slides, never clipped.
